Analogue watch used by Hol Horse from Jojo's Bizarre Adventure. Shake to show seconds and 3 custom complications; very configurable!

Tags: roman numerals, round, anime, manga, novelty, functional, ornate, fancy, efficient, japanese, 日本語, jjba

Configurable features:
    - Battery meter
    - Alarm indicator
    - Status icons:
        - Bluetooth disconnection (ゴゴゴ)
        - Quiet time (しーん)
    - Complications:
        - Battery %
        - Day name or number
        - Month name or number
        - Steps (count or distance)
        - Sleep (all or restful)
        - Alternate Timezone
    - Custom text (brand, monogram etc.)
    - Colours fully customisable (preset and custom themes)
    - Animation speed: Normal, Fast or Off
    - Complication visibility: Always, Never, or On Activity (for each individually)

Coming soon:
    - Weather, temperature, rain, uv index etc.
    - Live-preview colour customisation
