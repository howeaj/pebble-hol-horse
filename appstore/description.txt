Hol Horse's watch from Jojo's Bizarre Adventure. Shake to show seconds and 3 custom complications. Very configurable.

Tags: roman numerals, analogue, round, anime, manga, novelty, functional, ornate, fancy, efficient, jjba

Configurable features:
    - Battery meter
    - Complications:
        - Battery level
        - Day name or number
        - Month name or number
        - Steps
        - Sleep
        - Alternate Timezone
    - Colour (preset and custom themes)
    - Custom text (brand, monogram etc)
    - Animation speed: Normal, Fast or Off
    - Individually set each complication to be always visible, show on shake or disabled entirely.
